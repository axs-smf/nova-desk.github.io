# NOVA DESK

> **Modern workspace essentials.** Desks, ergonomic chairs, lighting and accessories designed for better work and focus.

NOVA DESK is a premium ecommerce concept focused on modern home-office and professional workspace equipment. The project combines contemporary furniture, ergonomic products, smart workspace accessories and carefully curated desk setups in one clean digital experience.

---

## ✦ Brand

**NOVA DESK Workspace**

NOVA DESK is built around a simple idea: a better workspace should feel as good as it performs.

The brand combines functional design, ergonomic thinking and modern technology to help customers create productive, comfortable and visually refined work environments.

## 🧩 Desk Builder

The **Desk Builder** is one of the core NOVA DESK concepts.

Customers can build a workspace by selecting:

1. Desk
2. Chair
3. Lighting
4. Monitor setup
5. Accessories
6. Cable management
7. Final workspace style

The builder can calculate an estimated setup total and provide a curated workspace configuration.

---

## 🧠 Workspace Finder

The **Workspace Finder** helps customers discover products according to their needs.

Example questions:

- How many hours do you work per day?
- How much space do you have?
- Do you prefer sitting, standing or both?
- Do you work with multiple monitors?
- Do you create content?
- Do you need a quiet professional setup?
- What is your preferred workspace style?

The result can recommend a complete setup instead of forcing the customer to select every product individually.

## 📸 Photography Standard

Product photography is an important part of the NOVA DESK identity.

Every product image should follow these rules:

- Realistic professional photography
- One product clearly visible
- Product completely framed
- Clean composition
- Consistent lighting
- No website screenshots
- No prices inside photographs
- No buttons inside photographs
- No stars or ratings inside photographs
- No product names inside photographs
- No badges inside photographs
- No interface elements inside photographs

All interface information belongs to the website UI, not inside the product photograph.

## 🔐 Security & Privacy

This project is a frontend ecommerce demonstration.

It does not store:

- Credit card information
- Real payment credentials
- Sensitive customer information

Any future production implementation should connect the storefront to a secure backend and a PCI-compliant payment provider.
